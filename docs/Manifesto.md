The ASM Manifesto

We believe observability should produce understanding, not merely data.

We believe confidence is as important as measurement.

We believe every metric should tell part of the operational story.

We believe engineers deserve evidence rather than assumptions.

We believe documentation is part of the implementation.

We believe deployment should be simple, repeatable, and portable.

We believe monitoring systems should be observable themselves.

We believe transparency builds trust.

We believe trust enables better operational decisions.

Observability without confidence is just telemetry.
